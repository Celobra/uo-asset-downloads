Pet progression

Develop your companion through combat and understand pet level bonuses.

A companion that grows

Use .petlevel to open pet progression. Pets can progress from level 1 to level 20 under the current server configuration.

A pet earns experience through meaningful combat participation. Bring it into the fight and check its progress through the pet panel.

Level bonuses

Each level above the first adds 5 Strength, 5 Dexterity and 5 Intelligence, along with 5 to the pet's health, stamina and mana caps.

Transferring a pet resets its progression. Take that into account before giving or trading a developed companion.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
