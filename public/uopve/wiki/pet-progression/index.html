Progression

Pet levels & combat perks

Train a permanent companion to level 20 through meaningful combat, then choose its level 10 and level 20 perks.

Inspect and train your pet

Type .petlevel and select one of your controlled pets on the same facet within 12 tiles. The training panel shows its level, accumulated experience, experience still needed and available special-skill choices.

Progression is enabled for owned controlled pets. Summoned and invulnerable creatures are excluded. Pets begin at level 1 and can reach level 20.

Earn combat experience

A qualifying defeated enemy awards a base of 10 pet experience per difficulty point. Your pet must personally contribute at least 10% of all recorded damage, and its last recorded damage must be no more than 120 seconds old.

At the enemy’s death, both you and the pet must be on its facet and within 24 tiles of it; you must also be within 24 tiles of the pet and alive. Each pet that meets the threshold can receive its own reward. The reward is not divided among qualifying pets after that check.

Valid prey are wild combat NPCs. Civilians, guards, players, pets, summons, owned and invulnerable creatures do not qualify. Animal- and human-brained prey must have negative karma.

Weak prey give less experience as a pet grows. If enemy difficulty is below three times the pet’s level, the reward is quartered. Otherwise, if it is below five times the pet’s level, the reward is halved. More challenging prey give the full base reward, with whole-number rounding and a minimum award of 1.

Attributes and resource pools

Each level after level 1 adds 5 strength, dexterity and intelligence, and 5 to maximum health, stamina and mana. Level 10 therefore contributes +45 to each; level 20 contributes +95 to each.

A level gain also restores up to 5 current health, stamina and mana, capped at their new maxima. Merely initializing an existing pet at level 1 does not add a free stat bonus.

Experience thresholds

Total experience to reach level N is 125 multiplied by the sum of the squares from 1 through N−1. Level 20 requires 308,750 total experience; the final step alone costs 45,125.

Choose special skills

Choose one perk at level 10 and another at level 20. A chosen slot cannot be changed through the training panel. The two choices can be from the same or different families.

Matching attack choices add to +30% scripted melee damage; matching defensive choices add to 30% damage reduction. Matching siphon choices add to 30% healing from the adjusted hit damage, with the combined heal capped at 30 per hit.

Siphon applies when attacking NPCs that are not players and have no owner. It does not provide this heal from hitting another player’s pet. Healing is capped at the pet’s maximum health.

Siphon has a combined maximum of 30 health per hit, with a minimum heal of 1 when it applies.

Hunter: Companion Bond

The Hunter’s Companion Bond talent adds 5% pet experience per rank and 1% pet melee damage per rank. At the configured 12-rank cap, that is +60% pet experience and +12% pet melee damage.

The experience bonus is applied after the weak-prey reduction. The damage bonus is combined with attack perks. It depends on the current owner’s Hunter talent, rather than being a permanent extra choice stored on the pet.

Ownership changes reset training

Changing a pet’s owner resets its progression to level 1 and zero experience, clears both special-skill choices and removes its recorded progression bonuses. Its original base attributes and maximum pools are restored.

Plan a transfer before investing in long-term training. A trained pet’s progression belongs to that owner-and-pet partnership; it is not carried intact to the new owner.
