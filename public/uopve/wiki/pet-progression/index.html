Progression

Pet levels & combat perks

Train a permanent companion to level 20 through meaningful combat, then choose its level 10 and level 20 perks.

Inspect and train your pet

Use .petlevel and select your own nearby controlled pet to inspect its twenty-level training path and choose abilities at levels 10 and 20. Summoned and invulnerable creatures are excluded. There is no daily training limit or deadline.

The menu shows advisory command load from one to five based on the creature’s original health, strength, magic skill and native slot definition. It is planning information: native follower-slot enforcement remains disabled, and opening the menu or levelling does not change actual follower costs.

Earn combat experience

A qualifying hostile creature supplies ten pet XP per difficulty point. Each qualifying pet receives its damage-based share of that pool. Two pets dealing half the recorded damage each split the pool; they do not each receive a full award. Your pet must contribute at least 10% of recorded damage, with its last eligible hit no more than 120 seconds old.

Prey must have difficulty at least 20 and at least three times the pet’s level. A level 20 pet needs difficulty 60 or higher. Weaker prey still provide ordinary loot but no pet-training XP. Civilians, guards, passive wildlife, players, owned creatures, summons and invulnerable creatures do not qualify.

At death, the pet must remain within 24 tiles of the victim. Its owner must be online, alive, on the same facet and within 24 tiles of both pet and victim. Recent owner damage against that victim qualifies; otherwise the owner needs an accepted movement, combat, spell, skill or pet-command action within 120 seconds. A fresh login or idle connection traffic does not count.

Each creature lifetime pays only once after an uncancelled death. Initial pacing depends on target difficulty, damage share and Hunter talents; it does not guarantee a fixed number of days to level 20.

Attributes and resource pools

Each newly earned level adds 2 to Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence and maximum health, stamina and mana. A newly trained level 10 pet therefore receives +18 to each; level 20 receives +38.

Existing five-point level gains are preserved. Future levels use the two-point increase, with the combined bonus tracked so transfers restore the correct original stats.

Experience thresholds

Total XP to reach level N is 1,000 multiplied by the sum of the squares from 1 through N − 1. Level 20 requires 2,470,000 XP; the final step costs 361,000. Existing pets retain earned levels and their fraction of progress through a one-time conversion.

Choose Striker, Guardian or Support

Choose one permanent role at level 10 and another at level 20. You may reinforce the same role or combine two. Matching Striker choices total +15% outgoing melee damage; matching Guardian choices total 15% incoming raw damage reduction.

Support heals the current owner using a percentage of the outgoing hit before target armor and damage mitigation. Both choices share a maximum of 15 HP per heal, a five-mana pet cost and a ten-second cooldown. The owner must be online, alive, injured, visible and within five tiles. Players, owned creatures, summons, invulnerable targets and passive wildlife cannot trigger it.

Previously learned damage, defense and self-leech choices retain their original 10% / 20% effects. New choices use the current roles below. A conversion or save does not grant a second choice.

Hunter: Companion Bond

The Hunter’s Companion Bond talent retains +5% pet XP and +1% pet melee damage per rank, reaching +60% XP and +12% melee damage at rank 12. Its experience bonus applies after the damage share.

The melee bonus combines with applicable pet attack perks and depends on the current owner’s Hunter talent. It is separate from the pet’s saved role choices.

Ownership changes reset training

Changing a pet’s owner resets its progression to level 1 and zero experience, clears both special-skill choices and removes its recorded progression bonuses. Its original base attributes and maximum pools are restored.

Plan a transfer before investing in long-term training. A trained pet’s progression belongs to that owner-and-pet partnership; it is not carried intact to the new owner.
