Classes

Choosing a class and spending talents

Permanent class choice, talent points, rank caps, refunds and active-talent rules.

Choose one of nine classes

Use .class to choose Druid, Hunter, Paladin, Warrior, Rogue, Warlock, Mage, Priest or Shaman. The confirmation menu makes this choice permanent for normal player use. These are custom character classes, separate from the classic skill-cap system.

Use .talents to allocate points. You earn floor(level / 2) points, capped at 50: first point at level 2, then one every two levels through level 100. Each class has five talents; each talent can reach rank 12. The full tree holds 60 ranks, so a 50-point character cannot maximize every talent.

Each + purchases one rank using one available point. Each − refunds one rank for 10,000 gold. Refunding all 12 ranks of one talent costs 120,000 gold. Refunding all 50 spent points costs 500,000 gold. Refunding points does not change your class.

Use an active talent

Use .talentuse 1 through .talentuse 5 for the corresponding slot. Passive talents activate automatically once they have ranks. Active talents cost 5 + rank resource points: 6 at rank 1, 17 at rank 12. Warrior/Rogue actives and Hunter slots 1, 2 and 4 use stamina; the other active classes use mana. Talent costs do not list spell reagents.

Successful active talents share a 5-second recovery. Their effect duration can be much longer. Targeted talents require a living character within 12 tiles on the same facet. Healing/cleansing/ward talents restrict targets to yourself, your controlled pet, a party member or a party member’s controlled pet.

Area offensive talents select unowned NPCs. Cleave, Smoke Bomb and Storm Totem further require negative karma; Turn the Unhallowed uses its undead/demon classification. Curse of Agony also requires an eligible hostile creature in sight. Other targeted offensive talents retain their own checks, so normal combat and region rules still matter.

Classes and spell schools

Warlock talents support Necromancy; Paladin talents support Chivalry. Their strength comes from spent talent ranks. There are no additional Necromancy or Chivalry skill gates, school multipliers or talent skill caps.

Learning a talent does not bypass a spell’s casting requirement. Necromancy controls casting checks and Spirit Speak supplies its spell power. Chivalry uses its own skill, positive karma and tithing points. Both schools have a base skill cap of 100.0 within the unchanged 1,200.0 total skill cap.

Warlock damage bonuses and Soul Leech apply to Pain Spike, Poison Strike, Strangle and Wither. Paladin Aura improves Close Wounds and Noble Sacrifice, and reduces Cleanse by Fire’s health cost. These talents do not add their school bonuses to Magery.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
