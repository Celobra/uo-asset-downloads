Character progression

Classes & talents

Choose a class and develop your character with levels and talent points.

Choose your class

Use .class to open class selection. There are nine choices: Druid, Hunter, Paladin, Warrior, Rogue, Warlock, Mage, Priest and Shaman.

Your confirmed class choice is permanent. Read the in-game descriptions before choosing, and use .cs to review your character sheet.

Levels and experience

The current character level cap is 100. Use .level or .xp to review your progress and .xpbar to show or hide the experience display.

Quests and hunting rewards are among the ways to develop your character. Consult the in-game character sheet for the effects of your current level and choices.

Spend talent points

Open .talents to review and spend your available points. The current progression grants one talent point every two levels, up to 50 points.

Read each talent before spending. The in-game panel is the current reference for requirements and effects as the server develops.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
