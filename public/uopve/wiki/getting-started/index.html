Getting started

Your first steps

Review the planned first steps while UO:PvE is in development.

Public access is not open yet

UO:PvE is still in development. Public play and launcher downloads are not available yet.

The wiki describes the developing realm. Follow the Updates page for future announcements.

Get to know your character

Type .cs in game to open your character sheet. Use .level or .xp to see your progression, and .xpbar to control the experience display.

Open .class to explore the available classes. Confirm only once you are happy with your choice: class selection is permanent. The Classes & talents guide explains the options.

Find a first quest

Visit the Hunter’s Guildmaster in Britain for the original supply deliveries, monster bounties and expeditions. The expanding realm also adds town deliveries, hunts and walking escorts: use Quest givers around Felucca to find the planned contacts and the complete quest catalogue to compare their jobs.

Read an offer at its named NPC and choose Accept quest, then use the Quests button on your paperdoll to follow progress. The journal shows accepted jobs only. Choose Track for a compact display, and return to the issuing giver when the objectives are complete.

For a longer journey, seek the Grave Warden at Britain Graveyard to begin the seven stages of The Undying March. Check .achievements for milestones and .titles for unlocked overhead titles.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
