Getting started

Your first steps

Install the client, enter the realm and find your first adventure.

Install and enter the realm

Download UOPvE-Setup.exe from the Downloads page and choose Install UO:PvE. Setup opens the launcher, which downloads ClassicUO and the game files. Allow about 2.2 GB for the initial download, plus room for updates.

Keep the launcher open until installation finishes. Select PLAY, then enter your shard account details inside ClassicUO. The launcher does not ask for your game password.

Get to know your character

Type .cs in game to open your character sheet. Use .level or .xp to see your progression, and .xpbar to control the experience display.

Open .class to explore the available classes. Confirm only once you are happy with your choice: class selection is permanent. The Classes & talents guide explains the options.

Find a first quest

Visit the Hunter's Guildmaster in Britain's guild district and open Guild Quests. Supply deliveries and monster bounties are a useful place to begin.

Accept a contract from the Guildmaster, then use the Quests button on your paperdoll to follow your progress. Opening the journal alone does not accept a quest. Return to the Guildmaster when its objectives are complete.

For a longer journey, seek the Grave Warden at Britain Graveyard to begin The Undying March. Check .achievements for milestones and .titles for unlocked overhead titles.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
