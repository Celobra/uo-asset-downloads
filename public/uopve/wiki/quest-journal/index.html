Adventuring

Quests & the journal

Find offers, follow your accepted quests, deliver supplies and guide an escort safely home.

Find and accept a quest

The developing quest library contains 100 one-time contracts: 35 gathering and delivery jobs, 45 hunts and 20 escorts. The complete quest catalogue lists every objective and reward; the Quest givers guide lists the new town contacts and their planned locations.

Double-click a quest giver within three tiles to see their offers, read the story and choose Accept quest. At the Hunter’s Guildmaster, open Guild Quests from his panel first. Reading an offer does not accept it. The paperdoll’s Quests button shows only quests you have accepted and have not yet handed in.

The Hunter’s Guildmaster offers the original ten supply deliveries, ten bounties and ten expeditions, with one Guild journal contract active at a time. New town jobs and The Undying March are separate from that Guild slot. The rotating weekly quarry is a separate, repeatable activity.

Read your journal and use Track

Select an accepted quest to see its issuing NPC, objectives, progress and rewards. Gathering, Hunts and Escorts filters help you find the job you need alongside the existing Guild categories.

Choose Track to open a compact live display for that quest. A supply job shows the first item still missing; a hunt shows the current stage and kill count; an escort shows its destination, coordinates and journey status.

The tracker is optional. Closing the journal keeps it closed until you open it again, and completed quests leave the active list. Hunt progress and completed escort arrival are saved; logging out interrupts a journey still in progress.

Deliver supplies or complete a hunt

Supply jobs accept items you gather, craft, buy or already own. Carry every requested item in your backpack and return to the giver. A delivery with several requirements checks the full order before consuming anything; successful hand-in consumes the exact required quantities.

Hunts require kill credit from the combat system and the listed creature type. Player victims, pets, owned creatures, summons and invulnerable NPCs do not count. For an objective shown as several stages, finish each stage in order: later targets do not advance the quest early.

The Undying March begins with the Grave Warden at Britain Graveyard. Its seven stages lead from zombies and skeletons through ghouls, wraiths or spectres, liches and lich lords to an ancient lich. It pays 5,000 gold and 1,500 XP.

Guide an escort on foot

Accepting an escort creates a walking companion for your character. Follow the destination shown in the journal and tracker, keep the companion close, and arrive together on Felucca. Only one walking escort can run per character.

A journey has a 30-minute limit. Staying more than 24 tiles apart for 10 seconds, changing facet, the death of you or your companion, or logging out interrupts it. Companions are vulnerable and carry no recoverable loot.

A failed journey stays in your journal. Return to its coordinator and choose Restart Escort to try again. Once your companion arrives safely, return to the issuing coordinator to collect payment; arriving alone does not finish the journey.

Collect your reward or abandon a job

Return within three tiles of the issuing NPC when the objectives are complete. Gold and Guild Marks go into your backpack, with your bank used if no backpack is available. If a reward cannot be placed, it remains waiting: make room and return.

Each journal contract pays once per character. The catalogue’s XP rewards apply while character progression is enabled. Guild expeditions retain their Guild Marks, weekly scoring and achievement progress.

You may abandon an unfinished quest that is not ready for payment. This clears that quest’s progress and lets you accept it again from its giver. A completed or handed-in objective with a waiting reward is protected from abandonment.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
