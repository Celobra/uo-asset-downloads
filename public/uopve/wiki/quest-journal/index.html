Quests & the journal

Track supply runs, bounties, expeditions and The Undying March.

Open your quest journal

Use the Quests button on the paperdoll to open your journal. It shows your tracked objectives and keeps progress between sessions.

Guild quests must be accepted from the Hunter's Guildmaster. Viewing a quest in the journal does not automatically accept it. Return to the Guildmaster to hand in completed Guild contracts.

Choose an adventure

The current quest set includes ten supply deliveries, ten monster bounties and ten expeditions, alongside The Undying March.

Supply contracts ask for goods, bounties send you after a particular enemy, and expeditions offer broader objectives. Read the contract's requirements and rewards before accepting.

The Undying March

Speak to the Grave Warden at Britain Graveyard. This seven-stage quest begins with zombies, then moves through skeletons, ghouls, wraiths and spectres, liches, lich lords and an ancient lich.

The stages follow a fixed order. The configured completion reward is 5,000 gold and 1,500 experience. Follow the journal for your current objective before moving to the next enemy.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
