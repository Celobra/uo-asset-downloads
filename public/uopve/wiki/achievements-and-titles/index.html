Achievements & titles

Follow your milestones, claim rewards and display an unlocked title.

Track your milestones

Type .achievements to open your achievement panel. Review the requirements, check your progress and claim rewards that are ready.

Questing and Hunter's Guild activities have achievement milestones, so revisit the panel as you complete contracts and hunts.

Wear an earned title

Use .titles to select from your unlocked overhead titles. Titles let other players see a milestone you have earned.

An achievement and its reward may require a claim before the associated title is available. Check the achievement panel first if an expected unlock is missing.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
