Progress, claims and titles

Use .achievements to review progress and claim unlocked prizes. Use .titles to choose an unlocked overhead title, and .rewards for level milestones. Achievement progress is saved on your character.

Qualifying combat achievements use hostile, untamed, unsummoned creatures and at least 10% damage credit. Some milestones count distinct families or locations rather than repeated kills. Read the requirement column for each entry.

An entry's target is the number or threshold checked for its metric. Its points are achievement points, separate from XP. A named keepsake uses the listed base item with an achievement-specific name and hue.

Rewards are delivered in a bank parcel. If the bank lacks capacity, the claim stays available. Achievement notices remain until dismissed. Entries that reference currently inaccessible facets remain catalog objectives and do not unlock those facets.
