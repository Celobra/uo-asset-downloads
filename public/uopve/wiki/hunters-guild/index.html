Adventuring

The Hunter's Guild

Accept contracts, join weekly hunts and spend your Guild Marks.

Meet the Guildmaster

Look for the Hunter's Guildmaster in Britain's guild district. Open Guild Quests to inspect and accept the supply deliveries, bounties and expeditions on offer.

Complete the objectives, track progress in your paperdoll's Quests journal and return to claim the contract's rewards.

The weekly quarry

The optional weekly hunt rotates among eight quarry types. A new hunting week starts on Monday at 00:00 UTC.

The Guild provides current and previous weekly top-ten boards. Check the current quarry with the Guildmaster before setting out.

Rewards and Guild Marks

Guild rewards include gold paid to your bank, experience and Guild Marks. Keep earning Marks to buy a field kit, Guild sash or trophy.

Guild milestones also contribute to achievements and unlockable titles. Open .achievements to review your progress and claim available rewards.

Guidance follows the current UO:PvE configuration. Use the in-game panels for the latest requirements and rewards.
