The Hunter's Guild: hunts & Marks

Every weekly quarry, reward modifier, board rule and item sold for Guild Marks.

Weekly hunts and credit

Speak to the Hunter's Guildmaster within three tiles and accept the weekly quarry. Progress survives logout. Finish your current quarry and claim any pending reward before accepting another. After hand-in, the current weekly quarry can be accepted again.

A hunting week begins on Monday at 00:00 UTC. The quarry rotates through the eight entries below, and the reward modifier rotates through three entries independently. Qualifying hostile kills require at least 10% damage contribution; pets, summoned or protected creatures and civilians are excluded.

An accepted old-week quarry can still be completed for its saved reward and modifier. It cannot add points to the new week's board.

The Guild also offers ten one-time supply deliveries, ten bounties and ten expeditions through the quest journal. You may hold one of those Guild journal contracts at a time; the new town jobs are independent of that slot.

The weekly top-ten board

The Guildmaster keeps current and previous weekly top-ten lists. Each handed-in weekly quarry adds its difficulty tier in points; Veteran's Hunt adds one more point.

Guild expeditions add their own tier in board points only if accepted and completed within the same week. Their one-time objectives and rewards are in the complete quest catalogue.

Spending Marks and funding supplies

Carry Marks in your backpack and speak to the nearby Guildmaster. A purchase checks capacity before spending them.

You can donate 10,000 carried gold for one Mark, up to five donation Marks per week. Donations do not add hunting board points.
