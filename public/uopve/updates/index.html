7 October 2026Client release

UO:PvE 1.0.4 makes character-creation text readable

Character names and starting-city descriptions now appear in white over the custom navy artwork.

  • Typed names, the caret and selected name text remain readable during character creation.
  • Close ClassicUO and reopen the launcher to receive the update automatically. Only one client file downloads, about 4.5 MiB.
  • Launcher 1.0.3 remains current. Existing players using it do not need to reinstall the launcher.
  • The corrected client was checked with ClassicUO alone and optional Razor; the custom artwork and game files are retained.

7 October 2026 at 20:58 BSTGameplay & player wiki

The complete player reference, plus class and loot fixes

The expanded wiki is available now. The gameplay fixes below take effect after the next server restart.

  • Expanded the player wiki to 79 guides with searchable tables for combat, crafting, quests, housing and world rules.
  • Added all 64 enabled Magery spells: mana, reagents, casting times, damage and healing curves, durations and resist checks.
  • Added weapon swing-time calculations, armour and parrying rules, healing, regeneration, death, all 49 enabled skills and all nine classes with 45 talents.
  • Added 1,136 creature and NPC entries with health, damage, skills, taming, difficulty ratings, abilities, loot and carving resources, plus pet care and progression.
  • Added all five treasure-map levels and dungeon-chest tiers, with skill requirements, guardians and rewards.
  • Added 1,078 crafting recipes, 31 ore types, 11 woods, all 45 Alchemy potions, food, fishing, wands, magic rings, enchantments and 1,368 weapon, armour and shield definitions.
  • Added all 555 achievements, 31 journal quests, weekly quarry hunts, invasions, shops, guilds, houses, storage upgrades, boats and travel.
  • Paladin Aura of Devotion now increases Heal and Greater Heal health restored by 2% per talent rank.
  • Mage Elemental Savant and Arcane Penetration, and Warlock familiar and next-spell bonuses, now increase actual spell damage as described in the class guides.
  • Warlock Pact Familiar now gains its caster as owner and expires after two minutes; the next-spell bonus is consumed once.
  • Corrected the 25% house storage-upgrade calculation for houses priced through their stored property value.
  • Corrected Meer mage scroll selections and green goblin wine drops.
  • Repaired treasure-map drops from eligible monsters and corrected reagent and artifact loot selections.

7 October 2026 at 19:15 BSTGameplay fixes

Combat, pets, housing and item fixes

Changes completed at the time shown. These changes take effect after the next server restart.

  • Pet attack and defence perks now apply their intended bonuses; custom pet healing continues reliably.
  • Corrected percentage-based class healing, Blood Sacrifice health costs and low-health Warrior bonuses.
  • Area attacks now hit eligible targets and credit damage to the attacking player.
  • Piercing Shot works with standard bows; corrected weapon, gathering and counterspell checks.
  • Party buffs and healing effects now expire correctly without older timers cancelling refreshed effects.
  • Improved talent cooldown and storm-effect handling when players disconnect and reconnect.
  • House security, decoration and item access now consistently respect owner, co-owner and friend permissions.
  • House sales validate the current listing and price, charge once and update ownership correctly.
  • Secret-chest password changes and unlocking now respect ownership, range and the existing attempt lockout.
  • Hair, beard and dye services validate selections before charging or changing your appearance.
  • Failed arcane-clothing conversions retain the gem; corrected arcane equipment and weapon-effect bonuses.
  • Corrected ammunition loading and unloading counts, and restored bola cooldown enforcement.
  • Moongates validate destinations correctly, and guild doors enforce membership access.
  • Fixed ore-mount material variants and the Hunter’s Guild board’s final entry.

UO:PvE 1.0.2 adds the custom client and login artwork

ClassicUO Standard Update4 uses the branded UO:PvE login screen, with optional Razor and more reliable updates.

  • Optional Razor Community Edition 1.9.77.0 includes matching GPL source and a compatibility build recipe.
