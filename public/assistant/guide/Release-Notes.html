UO Assistant 0.7.0

18 October 2026 · unsigned Windows x64 development preview

Pet healing and tool audit fixes, optional casting and targeting convenience, in-game command panels and original branding.

What changed

  • Healing uses the selected named patient, configured spell range and a patient fixed for each fresh target. Watches explain temporary range, health and mana waits.
  • Cure works for a poisoned patient at full health; cooldown estimates persist across restarted watches. Unsupported saved spell IDs are retained but cannot start healing.
  • Optional Free hands before casting and Restore weapon and shield after casting use observed item and cursor state. Remember/Restore hands also have explicit buttons and shortcuts.
  • Optional ordinary-potion hand preparation waits for cleared hands and restores originals after observed stack use or removal. Shard potion graphics and hand timing are editable.
  • Native pet, boat and custom command panels share named buttons with hotkeys and action bars. Add shard commands such as .sacall; saving or opening a panel sends no speech.
  • Smart last target keeps beneficial and harmful histories separate. Optional range and poisoned-heal checks keep a refused cursor available.
  • Convenience options include nearby doors/corpses, rotation, target range, dismount protection, incoming names, health, spell labels and buff timers.
  • Run and Preview now use visible edits in affected item/vendor tools, macro Repeat uses its visible value, and recording uses its visible options and folder.
  • Loot saving preserves AND conditions; equipment editing preserves property requirements and dispatch checks their exact revision. Unrelated partial facts no longer disable complete actions.
  • Fixed patient status starvation, old confirmation cursors, stale settings acknowledgements, vendor quantities, travel/resource checks and missing hand/layout actions.
  • New original UO Assistant icons cover the game, companion, launcher and installer. The main assistant remains separate; smooth GPU gameplay and paired closing are retained.

Using the new tools

Open Character / Health & pets / Healing to choose the patient and method. Open Character / Equipment / Casting & convenience for hand recovery and everyday choices. Open Controls / Commands for pet, boat and custom buttons. Save choices for each shard profile.

Verification

1,307 application logic checks across 12 suites, 502 actual synthetic companion interface checks across 58 destinations, 64 fenced native self-checks, 111 launcher checks and 15 launcher interface checks. Installer lifecycle tests use 18 assertions with isolated test identities; 13 installer source guards also pass. Package inventories, source correspondence, archive CRC, privacy and published downloads are checked separately.

The controlled pre-login GPU fixture is measured close to 60 Draw FPS. This measures completed presentation calls and CPU; it is not a live-world or physical-display benchmark. The updated offline guides contain 84 pages of full scripting reference, 21 pages of beginner scripting, 6 pages of getting started and 6 pages of healing/controls.

Limits and compatibility

  • Unsigned Windows x64 development preview.
  • Synthetic checks and a network-fenced pre-login GPU fixture do not prove live shard behavior, casting completion, boat commands or populated-world performance.
  • A cast that does not provide enough completion evidence leaves hands alone; use Restore remembered hands when appropriate.
  • Automatic helpers follow the shard permissions and use known owned items; they do not change server rules.
  • Existing installed applications, personal profiles, clients, accounts and shard saves were preserved during verification.
  • External Qodo review was unavailable because its CLI was signed out; local peer reviews are recorded separately.
  • Recording binaries and corresponding FFmpeg archives are unchanged from release 0.3.0; receiving hardware still needs a short recording test.

Existing scripts and profiles remain available. Downloads and installation are explicit actions. Setup replaces identified older installs and preserves user data. Earlier releases and dependency sources are retained.
