ONE ASSET, ALL ITS CONNECTIONS

Keep the creative work moving.

An item is more than a picture. It may need an inventory icon, animation, a paperdoll image, TileData, sound and a script. Studio lets you work on those connections together.

Select a definition and follow its artwork, related files and references. Import a new appearance, check how it moves, give it gameplay properties and review the changes before saving. You spend less time moving between disconnected tools and more time making the thing you imagined.

  1. 01

    Find & connect

    Search definitions, inspect native assets and find available space.

  2. 02

    Create & fit

    Import artwork and animations; connect icons, paperdolls and scripts.

  3. 03

    See & refine

    Compare frames, try hues and preview scale, movement and placement.

  4. 04

    Review & deliver

    Queue linked changes, check file access and export or save deliberately.

EQUIPMENT THAT BELONGS TOGETHER

From one blade
to a complete armor set.

Pair an inventory icon with the worn animation and male/female paperdoll images. Import a named piece folder or gather a complete set, then check its action coverage.

Add a Strength penalty, Luck, resistance or a skill bonus. Give a weapon a percentage chance to cast Flamestrike, Fireball and other spells on a hit. The generated Sphere script stays visible and editable.

Explore equipment and scripting →

SEE IT BEFORE YOU SAVE IT

A place to test
what you are making.

Load a character, mount, item, equipment piece or effect into an isometric room. Try directions and actions, place objects, add test walls and inspect scale, hue and lighting.

Preview queued changes and external VD/PNG/GIF files. The Test Scene is built into Studio; it does not run shard combat or execute Sphere triggers.

Step inside the Test Scene →

BUILD BEYOND THE ASSET

A cottage.
A dungeon. A new world.

Lay out floors, walls, roofs and decorations in House Builder, walk through the design and compile a native multi with a Sphere house and deed script.

Or start at world scale: generate coastlines, islands, deserts, icy regions, rivers, mountains and volcanoes. Reserve black space for connected dungeons and caves, then explore the terrain in a separate zoomable window.

THE REST OF YOUR WORKBENCH

The details have a home, too.

01 / ASSET WORK

Art & animation

Inspect items and TileData, browse available slots, import or export animation, and check directions and action coverage.

02 / PRESENTATION

Gumps, hues & sound

Inspect gumps, design Sphere dialogs, compare colors, audition sounds and see or replace their script associations.

03 / SCRIPTING

Definitions with context

Search Sphere definitions, follow references, edit scripts and place generated content into relevant script folders.

04 / PROJECTS

Profiles & continuity

Keep shard profiles, linked folders, favorites and recent assets together. Checkpoints and recovery help you resume work.

05 / DELIVERY

Conversion & content packs

Convert supported MUL/UOP families in either direction, bulk-export artwork and share supported changes as content packs.

06 / CONTROL

Review before writing

Inspect affected files, queue linked changes and use pre-save access checks. Review references before removing related content.
