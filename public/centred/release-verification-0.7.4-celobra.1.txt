Limits
Initial preparation must finish before a new tab is ready; larger maps require
more time, disk space and memory. Tab Undo/Redo is retained during the open
session; saved view settings persist across restarts. The editor uses supplied
UO artwork and creates separate working map copies.

The build is unsigned. Automated executable checks do not replace acceptance
on another computer or in-game testing of exported maps. The hands-on interface
review was stopped at the user's request; no final accessibility, large-font or
exhaustive interactive layout acceptance is claimed. Remaining proposals are
listed in AUDIT-RECOMMENDATIONS.md and are not advertised as implemented.
